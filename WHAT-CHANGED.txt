THIS ROUND
  * Fire can leech. So can every element. Measured first: SEVEN of eleven
    elements had no leech at all (fire, earth, air, electric, ice, void, light),
    TEN of eleven had no cleanse (only Light), and Electric could not mend.
    The "Wellspring batch" fills exactly those holes — 7 drains, 10 cleanses,
    1 mend — written per element, not recoloured from one template: the Fire
    drain is close work, the Air drain reaches across the field, and the
    cleanses differ in what they cost you.
  * Nobody is stuck standing still. Eight elements had no way to close a gap or
    break off; 11 movement verses fix that (Earth scree slide, Electric arc step
    and earthing roll, Ice glacier step, Light ducking its own glare, Spirit
    step, Normal sidestep, ghoul lurch and playing dead).
  * Control effects deliberately stay signature. Root is Earth's, Sleep Ice's,
    Gloom the Void's and the dead's. Spreading every control across every
    element would make them all feel the same — the opposite of the point — so
    the test asserts BOTH that everyone can look after themselves AND that no
    control has crept onto nearly every element.
  * Verses: an "Auto-level" button. Spends every point you have, always topping
    up whichever verse is furthest behind so the set comes up evenly. Loadout
    tab spends across your bar; Library across everything you know. Capped
    verses are skipped; if all are capped the points stay in your pocket.
  * Autoplay stops filling your Ghost Box. tryRecruit and bossGhost both had an
    autoplay branch that adopted every ghost, bypassing your keep setting — so a
    long unattended run came back with hundreds of ordinary ghosts. It keeps
    shinies only now; your own setting still applies when you are playing.
