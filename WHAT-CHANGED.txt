WHAT IT DOES
  * Autoplay enters dungeons. It used to walk to the FURTHEST dungeon marker on
    the map, award itself a flat 12 Adventuring, and stand there — it never went
    in. It now takes the nearest uncleared rift, walks to the door, goes
    through, clears it, and leaves.
  * Autoplay does quests. New "Quests" task: goes where the current step points,
    talks to who needs talking to, hands in when ready. Kill-N / gather-N steps
    have nowhere to walk to, so they are left to the tasks already feeding them.
  * Autoplay mines in TURBZ. "Mine ore" only knew about ore nodes, so in New
    Texture Mode — where ore comes from breaking rock — it did nothing at all.
    It now finds the nearest mineable block, gets in range and works it.
  * Travellers cross the TURBZ wild. The mode has no towns, so Social was the
    one skill with nothing training it. Up to three are out at a time; press E
    to talk. Each has one thing worth hearing, so Social comes from meeting
    people over a run rather than mashing a key at one person. Autoplay's
    "Talk" task seeks them out too.
  * Sound effects and music no longer share a compressor. Everything ran through
    one chain (ratio 8, 1.7x makeup), so every swing pumped the music and the
    music held the effects down. Now: effects get a glue compressor set for
    cohesion rather than loudness — soft knee, modest ratio, attack slow enough
    to keep the front of a hit — with NO makeup boost, then sit lower. Music
    runs straight past it. A transparent ceiling still catches peaks.

    3. a traveller turning up, and E talking to them
