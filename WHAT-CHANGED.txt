TURBZ — Class Update (V4 kits) + two autoplay UI fixes
======================================================

Drop these over the repo root; every path below is already repo-relative.


WHAT YOU ASKED FOR
------------------

1. REMIXED CLASS KITS — four starting verses, not seven.

   Every class now starts with exactly four: a main hit, a second trick, a ward
   or a mend, and a way to move. That was always the shape the kits were
   designed around (it is written at the top of class-kits.ts); five were listed
   and two more were appended automatically from the bonus "sx_" verses, which
   is how a starting loadout nobody chose ended up nearly filling the nine equip
   slots. That auto-append is gone.

   The bonus verses were NOT deleted. They are still in the game and still
   learnable at a shrine of their element — they are just no longer handed over
   on the first morning.

   Old saves: the kit version went 3 -> 4, so an existing hunter is handed any
   of her new four she is missing on next load. Nothing is ever taken away, so a
   loadout built up over a life survives the change.

2. 50/50 MELEE AND RANGED — 20 and 20.

   It was 12 melee / 24 "mixed" / 4 ranged. "Mixed" is gone from the fixed
   roster. Seven classes moved to close quarters because that is what they
   always sounded like and never played like:

     Nightlord    a Bruiser that only ever cast
     Outrider     a skirmisher with four melee verses already in its pool
     Vigilant     the night watch, now swinging the lantern it carries
     Hexbinder    pacts cut into them rather than drawn in the air
     Sovereign    the scepter turns out not to be ceremonial
     Seraph       it already had the wings, the dive and the blink
     Moonhopper   five of its seven verses were melee to begin with

   Those five that had no close-range verses got new ones written for them (17
   new kit verses in total, including the Engineer's). Their signature verse
   moved with them, so each still leads with its own move.

   The Crying Girl is deliberately excluded from the split — her fight style is
   whatever you build.

   This is enforced now: a melee class's attacking starters must land within
   melee reach, a ranged class's must not. See scripts/class-update.test.mjs.

3. STATS AND GRINDS.

   Every class now has TWO aptitudes — its main accelerated grind plus a second
   one it is also good at (Bulwark: Mining and Building; Corsair: Fishing and
   Trading; Arcanist: Magic and Mana). The "Grinds X" line on the character
   screen names both.

   Stat lines were revamped only for the seven classes that changed hands — a
   melee class with "-25 Might" on it was a bug, not a trade-off. Each one was
   rebalanced at EXACTLY its old stat budget, so nothing got stronger:

     stormrider 25 -> 25   vigilant 32 -> 32   hexbinder 9 -> 9
     nightlord  12 -> 12   seraph   27 -> 26   moonhopper 20 -> 20
     sovereign  47 -> 47

   Nothing else about any class moved. The field-level diff is: aptitudes,
   fightStyle, signature, specialty, starterMoves, stats — and nothing else.

4. THE ENGINEER.

   New class, 41st on the roster. Electric, Bruiser, melee, immune to Spark.
   Focus: Powered Machines. Grinds Building and Mining.

   Every machine she wires runs DOUBLED:
     - batteries and capacitors hold twice as much
     - HellStone generators, log burners and solar panels give twice as much
     - duplicators turn out two copies per cycle instead of one
     - the XP Producer pays double
     - Tesla coils bite twice as hard

   And the work those machines do feeds her directly: every few finished cycles
   — a copy made, a block laid, a lump of Power generated — hands her a little
   experience, so a well-laid camp levels her while she is standing in it.
   Nobody else gains anything from this.

   Her R is OVERCLOCK: for 10s the whole grid runs doubled AGAIN on top of her
   own doubling (so x4), and the air around her arcs into anything close.

   Her kit: Wrench Swing, Piston Punch, Coolant Ward, Jet Boot.
   Her perks: Surge Tap, Grounded Boots.
   Her sprite is painted into the last free block on the warrior sheet by
   scripts/sprites/engineer-class.py (idempotent — rerun it any time).

   One thing worth knowing: stored Power is now sanitised against the largest
   multiplier any class can apply, not the base one. Without that, loading an
   Engineer's save would have quietly emptied her capacitors down to 500.

5. AUTOPLAY UI — the two things you flagged.

   - The tick no longer turns into a cross when a task is switched off. The box
     is simply empty, so the switch reads as unchecked. The box keeps its width
     so the label does not shuffle sideways.
   - (The "clear push when something is disabled" and the Auto-level fix from
     the last drop are included here too, since those files are in this zip.)
