TURBZ — UI / menu overhaul (interaction + QOL)
==============================================

Drop these over the repo root; every path below is already repo-relative.

NOTE: this zip is self-contained. It also carries the class-update files from the
last drop, at their current state, so it does not matter whether you applied that
one first. Nothing here undoes it.


WHAT I FOUND FIRST
------------------

Right-click already existed, but it was really a menu dump: right-clicking
anywhere on the canvas opened the thing's description plus the SAME thirteen
buttons every time (Bag, Skills, Quests, Map, Verses, Spirits, Crafting, Ghost
jar, Bounty, Trade, Pause, Autoplay). It did nothing at all on bag items, gear,
verses, spirits or perks.

Hover was split in two. The canvas had a decent rich card for foes. Every panel
in the UI fell back to the browser's own `title=` tooltip — slow to appear,
unstyled, and truncated exactly when the text mattered.

Worth saying plainly: the bag, verses, spirits and skills panels ALREADY had
search, sort and filters. I did not rebuild those.


1. ONE MECHANISM FOR BOTH (src/components/ui-overlay*.ts/tsx — new)
-------------------------------------------------------------------

   hoverProps(content)   gives any element a real hint card
   menuProps(menu)       gives any element its own right-click menu
   targetProps(m, h)     both at once, merged properly
   <UiOverlay />         rendered once near the root, draws whichever is open

Details that matter:

  - Hints appear after 110ms so they do not flicker as the cursor crosses a
    list, follow the cursor once up, and never take the pointer — you can
    always click the thing you are reading about.
  - Cards flip to the other side of the cursor rather than running off an edge,
    and a card bigger than the screen is pinned on rather than pushed off.
  - A menu closes on Escape, on a click elsewhere, on scroll and on resize.
    Arrow keys walk it, Enter picks, and the first item takes focus on open.
  - Touch gets the menu from a long press (450ms), cancelled if the press turns
    into a scroll. Hint cards are hidden on touch entirely — a hint you cannot
    hover is just a card that appears when you try to tap something.
  - Opening a menu takes any hint down, and a hint will not open over a menu.
  - A row that HAS a menu shows a small corner mark on hover, so right-click is
    discoverable rather than a secret.

I split the store and the placement maths into a plain .ts file with no React
import (ui-overlay-core.ts) specifically so they could be tested — see below.


2. RIGHT-CLICK, EVERYWHERE
--------------------------

  Bag item      Use · Set to Space · Queue · Take off Space · Sell one ·
                Sell ten · Sell all
  Gear          Wear (with the score difference against what you wear) ·
                Open in detail · Lock/Unlock · Sell
  Verse (bar)   Upgrade · Move to another key · Open options · Unequip
  Verse (list)  Put on the bar · Upgrade
  Perk          Spend 1 · Spend 5 · Take it to the cap
  Canvas        the things you can actually do here (see 3)

One thing I was careful about: gear sitting in a CHEST or in a loot pick is not
yours yet, so Wear/Lock/Sell there would be acting on something you do not own.
Those cards get the hover read and no menu.

Because the actions moved onto the thing itself, the cards shed buttons — the
bag card went from four to one or two. That is most of why the bag read as a
wall.


3. THE CANVAS MENU IS NOT A MENU DUMP ANY MORE
----------------------------------------------

What is under the cursor is now the menu. The thirteen shortcuts are still
there, folded behind "More", shut by default. If there is genuinely nothing to
do where you clicked, it opens on the shortcuts, because that is plainly what
you wanted.


4. HOVER READS — "can't tell what things do"
--------------------------------------------

  Bag item   what it does, its effect, why you can't use it, worth each and
             worth the lot, and whether it is on the Space bar
  Gear       level, slot, quality, element, score, EVERY enchantment spelled
             out, the set bonus, the FX line, its value — and how it stands
             against the piece you wear in that slot
  Verse      tactic, power/heal/ward, accuracy, crit, range, cooldown, effect,
             the area note, the description, and what the next rank is worth
  Perk       the FULL text (the card only ever showed the first sentence) and
             what each point adds
  Skill      what it does for you and the XP to the next level


5. FIND ANYTHING — Ctrl+K / Cmd+K (src/components/find-palette.tsx — new)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every big menu already had its own search, which was the problem: to find
something you first had to know which of five menus it lived in. Ctrl+K searches
all of them at once — menus, bag items, gear, verses, skills, perks, spirits —
and takes you to it. Arrow keys, Enter, Escape.

It only ever NAVIGATES. It opens the menu that holds what you picked and gets
out of the way. It cannot spend a point, sell a piece or equip anything,
because a fuzzy search box is the last place that should be able to.

Performance note: it is mounted the whole session, so the shell watches only
`mode`. The part that reads the bag, the gear and every verse is mounted only
while the palette is open — otherwise the game would re-render against all of
those arrays for the entire session.


6. DENSITY
----------

Deliberately a measured loosening, not a restyle (you picked "keeps the current
pixel look"):

  .ui-card      padding 7/9 -> 9/11, gap 4 -> 5
  .ui-card-about  17px -> 18px, tighter leading relaxed
  .ui-grid      gap 8 -> 10
  .ui-section   gap 8 -> 10
  .ui-stack     gap 12 -> 14

BE AWARE: I cannot run vite in this environment, so these spacing numbers are
the one part of this drop I could not look at. They are intentionally small so
they are easy to nudge — all five live together in src/styles.css.
