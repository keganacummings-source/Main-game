THE TWO BUGS YOU REPORTED
  * Auto-level did nothing with 200 points. The loop was fine; the save shape
    broke it. On a save with no `loadout` array, the first never-before-ranked
    verse hit `save.loadout.length` and threw part way through — so the button
    did nothing AND said nothing. It now repairs what it needs first, and when
    it genuinely cannot spend it tells you which case it is (no points, no
    verses, or everything at the top rank — and in that case the points are left
    for you rather than eaten). The old code claimed "every verse is at the top
    rank" whenever it bailed for any reason, which would have been a lie here.
  * The autoplay OFF state was not readable. An off task was just an
    un-highlighted button, which reads as "not selected" rather than "off".
    Off tasks now show ✕ instead of ✓, are struck through and greyed, lose their
    place number (they are not in the running order) and are badged OFF in red.

NEW: WEATHER AND DUAL-ELEMENT VERSES
  * Conditions a verse can call down: Summon Rain (soaks everything, feeds water,
    smothers fire), Sandstorm (scours, spoils every aim), Void Blackout (glooms
    and gnaws), Bewilder (foes swing at nothing), Heatwave, Call the Gale.
    One holds at a time. Each feeds its own element and damps the opposite, and
    most of them touch YOU as well — calling rain to feed your tide verses soaks
    you too. Three more call a condition AND land in the same motion.
  * Dual-element verses: a verse can follow through with a second element as a
    lighter second hit, read against the foe's resistances on its own. So fire
    into ice genuinely beats something that only shrugs off one of them.
  * 23 of the duplicate verses now carry a second element, each different from
    its neighbours, so they are no longer interchangeable.
