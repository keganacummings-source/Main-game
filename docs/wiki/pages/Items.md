# Items

Everything that goes in the **Bag** that isn't gear. Use an item from the Bag.

## In short

- **Potions** heal in TurboHunt. They scale with your max health, so they stay useful at every level. In Hardcore they **don't heal**; only healing verses do.
- **Ghost Jars** make foes far more likely to stay as spirits for 60 seconds.
- **Cleanse Salt** clears every harmful effect and leaves immunity to sleep, root and silence for a few seconds.
- **Brews** give 25-second buffs.

## Using items

| Item | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Potion, Big Potion, Spirit Candy, food** (TurboHunt) | Heal max(listed amount, max health × listed ÷ 300) × (1 + Deep Sip %). A Potion heals at least 15% of your max health, a Big Potion at least about 37%. |
| **Warmth Tonic** | Heals 70 (TurboHunt), then 25 s: fire verses +22% damage (counts as matching element gear). |
| **Cat's Eye Draught** | 25 s: +20 crit chance. |
| **Ironroot Phial** | 25 s: 20% less damage taken (counts toward the 45% Ward cap). |
| **Ghost Jar** | 60 s: +45% catch chance. |
| **Cleanse Salt** | Clears all harmful effects (immunity after sleep, root, silence). Two pinches also complete *Salt Run*. |
| **Smoke Bomb** | Steps you back out of melee. |
| **Green Mushroom** | Hardcore, level 30+: wake in a fresh Green Cave. |
| **Food and drinking water** (Hardcore) | Restore hunger or thirst. See [Hardcore Survival](Hardcore-Survival). |

Raw meat and raw fish can't be eaten in Hardcore: cook them first. Materials (ore, logs, leather, reagents) are used in [recipes](Gathering-and-Crafting) or sold.

## Where items come from

- **Monster drops:**
  - 22% + 1.2% per level + 6% per rarity (at most 80%) for 1–2 food items: raw fish from Water foes, shrooms from Earth foes, raw meat otherwise;
  - 8% + 0.8% per level + 5% per rarity (at most 55%) for a **Cleanse Salt**;
  - Epic foes have 12% + 0.4% per level for a **Ghost Jar**.
- **Animals:** meat and leather.
- **Bounties:** a full bag. See [Bounties](Bounties).
- **Shops:** Quartermaster Holt and the [Black Market](Economy).
- **Story quests.** See [Quests](Quests).
