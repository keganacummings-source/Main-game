# Game Modes

From the hub you can pick:

| Mode | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **TurboHunt** | The main game. Explore, fight, take bounties, climb the ladder. Forgiving: health regenerates and dungeon deaths only knock you out. |
| **Hardcore** | One life. Hunger, thirst and rest, nights that double enemy strength, building, and the Green Cave. Enemies hit harder and keep growing. |
| **Last Man Standing** | You against 99 rival witches on Veilreach in an endless night. Everyone starts at level 1 under Hardcore rules; the last one alive wins. |
| **TurboCircle** | A battle simulator. **Classic** is 6 v 6 on any map; **War** is two armies of 10–100 a side on the large Battlefield map. |
| **Leaderboards** | The grave ledger: the top 100 fallen witches in Hunt and Hardcore, ranked by that life's score. Search, sort, and open a name for the kit. |
| **Designer Mode** | The Map Builder and the Enemy Designer. See [Designer Mode](Designer-Mode). |
| **Options** | Account and friends. |

TurboHunt and Hardcore keep **separate saves**. Your account-wide progress (skills, account level, perks and Account Score) is shared. See [Death and Account](Death-and-Account).

## Last Man Standing

- **Setup:** pick a name and class. You and 99 rivals spawn evenly spread across the 500×500 Veilreach map. Townsfolk, quests and portals are gone; only resource nodes remain.
- **Rules:** Hardcore (one life, hunger, thirst, rest, no auto-equip, loot chests). It is always night and the only enemies are ghosts. Sleeping restores rest but never skips to day. You can swim.
- **Rivals** level up, spend skill points (a heal, then a ward, a dodge, then attacks), gather, cook, brew potions and gear up. Once a rival has a full set and is level 14 or higher it starts hunting other witches, you included; after 18 minutes everyone hunts.
- **Fighting a rival:** they kite at their best range, heal and ward when hurt, drink potions, dodge and flee to recover. A fallen rival leaves a loot chest.
- **Winning:** be the last witch standing. The HUD shows how many are left, your kills, match time and the nearest rival.
