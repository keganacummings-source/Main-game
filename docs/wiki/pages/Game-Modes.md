# Game Modes

From the hub you can pick:

| Mode | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **TurboHunt** | The main game. Explore, fight, take bounties, climb the ladder. Forgiving: health regenerates and dungeon deaths only knock you out. |
| **Hardcore** | One life. Hunger, thirst and rest, nights that double enemy strength, building, and the Green Cave. Enemies hit harder and keep growing. |
| **TurboCircle** | A battle simulator: pick a map, plant teams of witches and spirits, and watch them fight. |
| **Leaderboards** | The grave ledger: the top 100 fallen witches in Hunt and Hardcore, ranked by that life's score. Search, sort, and open a name for the kit. |
| **Designer Mode** | The Map Builder and the Enemy Designer. See [Designer Mode](Designer-Mode). |
| **Options** | Account and friends. |

TurboHunt and Hardcore keep **separate saves**. Your account-wide progress (skills, account level, perks and Account Score) is shared. See [Death and Account](Death-and-Account).
