# Enchantments

Enchantments are the bonus lines on gear. A piece can hold up to 20. The same enchantment on several pieces **adds up**, and most can also be bought as permanent account **perks**. See [Leveling and Skills](Leveling-and-Skills#perks).

## In short

- **Weapon** enchantments boost damage: Might, Crit Edge, Double Strike, Keen Edge, Spellweave…
- **Armor** enchantments keep you alive: Ward, Plating, Vitality, Echo Guard, Thorns…
- **Relic** enchantments are utility: Focus, Deep Sip, Fortune.
- **Anywhere** enchantments help the economy and skills: Loot Find, XP Boost, Kyo Magnet, Harvest, Fish Luck…
- Many have a **cap** on the combined total (Double Strike 85%, Ward 45%, Echo Guard 40%…).

## How strong a roll is

A roll's power is a share of the enchantment's maximum. That share grows with the piece's **level** and **quality**:

- **Percent** enchantments: 16% of max at level 1, up to 74% at level 1000, plus up to 20% for quality.
- **Flat** enchantments: start tiny and grow with the square of the level, so they matter most at high levels.

Every roll varies ±12%.

| Enchantment | Cap |
| --- | --- |
