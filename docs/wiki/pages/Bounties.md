# Bounties

Bounties are rival witches with a price on their heads. They are the best source of high-level gear in the game.

## In short

- Every **tavern** has a **bounty board** (walk to it and press **E**) with **3 postings**. You can hunt all three at once.
- Each kill posts a **stronger** mark: the tier keeps climbing.
- A mark gives you **5 minutes** to come to her. After that she **enrages** (far stronger) and hunts you down, even through doors.
- Bounties hit hard. Every few seconds she lays a **Death Mark**: a red ring on the ground under you, then a blow worth over three normal hits. Step out of the ring, blink, roll, dash or ward in time.
- When she falls, you get **all her kyo and her whole bag**, and in TurboHunt the game pauses while you **choose one piece** of her six-piece kit.
- After your first bounty, ordinary kills can earn **Bounty Stars**. At **10 stars** you become **Wanted**, and squads of **5 hunters** come after you until you clear your name.

## Taking a bounty

Each posting shows the mark's name, class and tier. Take one and she appears somewhere on your map with her **spirits**. A message tells you how many tiles away and in which direction, and the HUD tracker follows her.

A mark:

- is a real class (with its signature verses) at **your level + 3 + tier**;
- has **3–7 traits** and **2–3 ghosts** (real ghosts — same ghost kits, aid verses that heal and ward her, the same shiny roll);
- waits at her spot and turns to face you when you come within 200 px;
- after **5 minutes** **enrages**: ×2.6 health, about ×2.1 damage, faster attacks and more frequent Death Marks, better crits and dodge, and she chases you across maps. Her ghosts enrage with her.

Once enraged and you are far away, she leaps toward you every couple of seconds.

### Tiers

| Tier | Level offset | Base damage | Health |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | +4 | 12% per clean hit (of your health) | Standard |
| 3 | +6 | Climbing with tier | Higher |
| 5 | +8 | Climbing with tier | Higher |
| 7+ | +10+ | Climbing with tier | Higher |

Her health and damage also grow with the usual [enemy growth](Enemies#growth). In Hardcore: ×1.2 health, ×1.35 damage, +1 extra trait.

## Death Mark

Every few seconds the bounty picks a spot at your feet and shows a red ring. Dodge it by:

- walking out of the ring;
- **blink**, **lunge** or **dash** out;
- **roll** clear;
- or putting up a **ward** in time.

A clean hit from Death Mark can kill. Failing to dodge it when enraged almost certainly will.

## The reward

When she falls:

1. **Kyo:** 60 + 22 × your level × (1 + 0.15 × tier) + 40 × tier.
2. **Her whole bag:** 2–4 potions (+1 per 3 tiers), 1–2 big potions (+1 per 4 tiers), cleanse salt, spirit candy, ghost jars, smoke bombs, leather, mushrooms, ore, timber and alchemy reagents. In Hardcore: also rations, cooked meat and drinking water.
3. **The pick (TurboHunt):** the world **pauses** and a loot window shows her six pieces (one per slot), rolled at your level with a +5% + 2% per tier bonus. Choose one. The window can't be skipped, and reloading reopens it.
4. **Hardcore:** no pause, no pick. Her bag and all six pieces drop in a **loot chest** where she fell. See [Hardcore Survival](Hardcore-Survival).
5. Hunting experience and account experience.

Her board posting is replaced by a new, higher tier.

## Bounty Stars

After your **first** bounty kill, every ordinary kill may drop a ★ Bounty Star:

| Condition | Star drop chance |
| --- | --- |
| Base | Increases with level |
| Per rarity step | +0.8% |
| Cap | 50% |

## Wanted

At **10 stars**, the stars reset and your **Wanted level** goes up by one. From then on:

- A **squad of 5 hunters** spawns about 9 tiles away a few seconds later.
- Hunters are random classes at **your level + 3** or higher, with **2+ traits** (more at higher Wanted levels).
- When a squad is wiped, the next comes **90 s** later (**60 s** in Hardcore).
- Hunters **follow you** through doors and rifts after about 4 seconds.
- Each hunter drops kyo, a potion or two, maybe a cleanse salt, and has a **35% chance** to drop a gear piece.

### Clearing your name

At any bounty board, pay to set Wanted back to 0, clear your stars and send the hunters home. Cost scales with your Wanted level.

A new witch always starts with a clean name.

## Tips

- Take **several postings at once**: they each wait 5 minutes, so you can chain them.
- Kill the **spirits first** if the mark is a healer or tank.
- The 5-minute timer is generous. Use it to heal, swap gear or prepare.
- Wanted squads drop decent gear. Some players stay wanted on purpose.
- In Hardcore, all her loot is in a chest — you have 5 minutes to loot it before it vanishes.
