# Gear FX

Good gear shows. Every **Fine-or-better** piece you wear adds its own animated effect around your witch.

## In short

- Every gear piece has its own hidden **FX gene**, so two pieces of the same element rarely look alike.
- The Gear Inventory's detail panel names each piece's effect — for example **"Comet · Void accent · star · flare"**.
- Each slot owns a part of the body. Ordinary pieces show a small pattern at their **own spot** rather than all orbiting the chest.
- Higher quality means more particles and a bigger ground ring. Legendary pieces often add a pulsing **flare**.
- Full sets add an **aura** with their own tint, size, spin and crest.

## What the gene decides

| Part | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Pattern** | orbit, comet, helix, sigil, shards, halo, wisps, petals, spiral, pulse, nova, tendrils, rune, swarm, ribbons, flicker, aurora, void rift… (24 patterns) |
| **Particle shape** | 8 shapes |
| **Accent element** | A second element's colour mixed in (more likely on better quality) |
| **Speed, spin, size, density, height** | Each varies per piece |
| **Flare** | A pulsing star: 12% chance on Rare, 35% on Epic, 70% on Legendary |

## How it adds up

- The **strongest** piece decides your ground ring's colour, gaps and spin.
- **Epic** quality and better adds a second ring and your element's signature motif.
- **Legendary** adds a third ring.
- Each Fine+ piece draws its own pattern in its **slot's own area**: head (above), chest (torso), legs (lower), feet (below), relic (side orbit), weapon (glint and timing).
- The busiest outfits are capped so the game stays smooth. Only Epic+ outfits get one quiet ground ring.

## Slot signatures

Rare (45%), Epic (75%) and Legendary (always) pieces roll their slot's signature in the piece's element and accent:

| Slot | Signature |
| --- | --- |
| **Chest** | Wings: feathered, demon, crystal, spirit, moth or void |
| **Legs** | Tail: demon, flame whip, wisp or orb chain |
| **Feet** | Trail: afterimages, footprints, ribbon or spark wake |
| **Head** | Crown: halo, horns, shard crown or third eye |
| **Relic** | Familiar: orb, star or moon |
| **Weapon** | Imbue: adds an extra layer to every verse you cast (echo twin, extra burst, sigils, comet copy) |

### Wings — what changed

The four original wing styles have been sharpened and extended:

- **Feathered** — barbs now alternate accent colour; the outermost tip gets a rare glint frame.
- **Demon** — the primary spar carries a travelling pulse of light along its vein.
- **Crystal** — thin lattice connector lines run between each shard position.
- **Spirit** — soft motes now drift upward off each wing tip.

Two new styles were added:

- **Moth Wings** — broad rounded membranes with a scattered scale pattern and an eyespot on the lower wing.
- **Void Wings** — inky black fills with drifting light rifts that bleed through from the inside out.

## Pet

A tiny companion based on your custom sprite may appear alongside your witch. The pet wanders nearby on its own, following you loosely rather than staying fixed to your body.

**Spawn chance is 5%**, rolling once when your gear set loads (only on Rare+ pieces). It is stable — your pet stays or stays away for as long as you wear the same gear. Swapping a key piece rerolls the chance.

## Set auras

Each of the 100 sets has its own variant of its aura kind:

- a **colour tint**, **size**, **mirrored spin** and one of seven **crests**:
  - orbiting glyphs;
  - a ground rune;
  - rising sparks;
  - twin halos;
  - a crown of points;
  - falling motes;
  - none.

The set's **pulse** also bursts in its own pattern, colour and sound. See [Sets](Sets).

## Ghost FX

Your ghosts and enemy witches' ghosts also have their own FX, read from the ghost itself:

- **Element** particles and colour.
- **Rarity**: particle count, crest and glow intensity.
- **Level**: particle spread.
- **Speed**: pace and a speed streak.
- **Health**: a guard ring.
- **Shiny**: rainbow lava-lamp colours, livelier movement.

Rare+ and shiny ghosts **imbue** their own verses with an extra visual layer on each cast. The Spirits window describes each ghost's FX.

## Verse visuals

Every verse also has its own look when cast:

- a **cast flourish**;
- an **impact flourish** (pillar, shockwave, shards, cross, sigil, implosion, spike crown);
- an **accent colour**, projectile size and trail tint;
- sometimes a **companion** that spirals around the shot;
- a **status overlay** showing what it does: sleep Zs, rising roots, a silence rune, a flame ring, a splash, gloom smoke, an "exposed" target marker, sparks, or drain motes flowing back to the caster.
