# Races and Traits

## Races

Your race comes from your class's element, and it decides which **armor sets** you see most. 62% of armor drops come from your race's 20 sets, and the rest from any race's.

<!-- gen:races -->

Electric and Air classes count as **Rime**. Light, Spirit, Ghoul, Void and Normal classes count as **Vesper**.

## Traits

A trait is an optional trade-off chosen when you make a witch. Its stats add to your class's.

<!-- gen:traits -->

Trait immunities (Unshakable, Survivor) stop the effect from landing at all (**IMMUNE**). See [Status Effects](Status-Effects).
