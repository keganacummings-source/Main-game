# Welcome to the TURBZ Wiki

TURBZ is a pixel-art action RPG. You play a witch hunting yokai across a ruined kingdom: fight in real time with **verses** (your attacks and spells), collect gear and armor sets, catch spirits that fight beside you, take bounties, and climb an account that outlives every witch you lose.

This wiki has two layers:

- **The basics** at the top of every page, written so a new player can learn the game.
- **Under the hood** sections with the exact formulas, caps and hidden rules, taken straight from the game's code.

The tables (verses, classes, enemies, items, sets, enchantments and more) are **generated from the game itself** and rebuild every time the game updates, so they are always current.

## The game at a glance

- Dozens of playable **classes** across six roles, each with its own element, stats, passive and starting verses.
- Hundreds of **verses** across 11 elements, each with its own tactic (strike, heavy, channel, blink, lunge, roll, aura and more).
- Gear from level 1 to 1000, in six qualities, with dozens of **enchantments** in seven categories and 100 **armor sets**.
- Enemies that **scale to you live**, with their own stats, traits and move kits — never below your level, never fixed.
- **Bounties**, **Bounty Stars** and **hunter squads** for players who want a real fight.
- **Hardcore**: one life, hunger, thirst, camp building, loot chests, nights that make everything twice as strong, and the hidden Green Cave.
- **Last Man Standing**: you vs 99 AI rivals on Veilreach, Hardcore rules, always night, everyone starts at level 1.
- **Every new life gets its own 500×500 generated world** — or keep the last one.
- **No level caps, no skill caps, no rank caps.** Everything bends instead of stopping.
