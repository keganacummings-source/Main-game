# Gear

## In short

- Six slots: **weapon, head, chest, legs, feet, relic**.
- Gear always drops at **your level**. Better quality and better sources (bosses, bounties) push it a little higher.
- Six **qualities**: Worn, Common, Fine, Rare, Epic, Legendary. Higher quality means more stats, more enchantments and a higher level.
- Each piece has an **element**: it adds damage to matching verses, and your chest's element decides which enemy hits hurt more.
- Each piece also rolls a **purpose** (shown in its name: *Gatherer's*, *Lucky*, *Farshot*, *Warded*, *Spirit-bound*, *Wanderer's*) and draws most of its enchantments from that purpose's category.
- **Gear Inventory** (**I**) is its own window. The **Bag** (**B**) holds consumables and materials only.

## Slots and stats

| Slot | Gives |
| --- | --- |
| **Weapon** | Weapon damage = 8 + 2 × level + 6 × quality rank + Whetstone. Adds 35% of its damage to melee and ranged verses. +28% to verses of its element. |
| **Head, chest, legs, feet** | Armor = 8 + 2 × level + 5 × quality rank + Plating. +8% each to verses of their element. |
| **Relic** | Armor = 3 + level ÷ 2 + Plating. +8% to verses of its element. |

Quality rank: Worn 0, Common 1, Fine 2, Rare 3, Epic 4, Legendary 5.

Armor pieces (not weapons) belong to an **armor set**. Wear all five pieces of one set for its bonus. See [Sets](Sets).

**Gear score** (used for auto-equip and Account Score) = weapon damage × 4 + armor × 3 + (power + 12) for each enchantment.

## Quality

| Quality | Enchantments from quality | Enchantments from level (max) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Worn | 0 | up to 5 | — |
| Common | 1 | up to 7 | — |
| Fine | 2 | up to 9 | FX active on worn pieces |
| Rare | 4 | up to 11 | May have a slot signature (45%) |
| Epic | 6 | up to 13 | Signature chance 75%; second FX ring |
| Legendary | 8 | up to 15 | Signature always; third FX ring; flare (70%) |

## Gear level

A drop's level is **your level**, raised by its quality and its source. It never builds on the gear you already wear.

**Level = your level × (1 + quality % + source bonus + luck) + quality flat**

- Luck is at most +5%.
- Levels run from 1 to 1000.

### Source bonuses

| Source | Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary foe | 0% |
| Boss | +10–14% |
| Bounty (by tier) | +5% + 2% per tier |
| Shrine | +8% |
| Evil witch | +12% |
| Black Market | +0–10% |
| Hunter | +3% |
| Stitching | luck from account level (12 + 2 × account level) |

High-tier bounties are the long road to level-1000 gear.

## Enchantments

Each piece rolls **0–20 enchantments**: up to 12 from level and up to 8 from quality (Worn 0 … Legendary 8), ±1 at random.

Weapons favour Combat enchantments; armor favours Defense; relics favour utility. The piece's **purpose** draws most of its rolls from the matching category. Enchantment power grows with level and quality. See [Enchantments](Enchantments) for every one.

**Disenchanting:** for 80 kyo, strip the last enchantment off the selected piece into your **stash**. Apply a stashed enchantment to another piece (up to 20 per piece). If the piece already has that enchantment, the powers add together up to its maximum.

## Where gear comes from

| Source | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Monsters** | Chance = 12% + 0.3% per your level (up to level 100) + 8% per rarity + (Loot Find + Fortune) ÷ 200, at most 85%, × the foe's loot multiplier. Quality floor rises with rarity and at your levels 24 and 40. |
| **Bosses** | Always one Fine (Rare above level 14) piece plus one Epic-or-better piece. |
| **Shrines** | A Rare-or-better piece of the shrine's element. |
| **Evil witches** | Three Epic-or-better pieces. |
| **Bounties** | In TurboHunt: choose one of six (Rare to Legendary by tier). In Hardcore: all six drop in a chest. See [Bounties](Bounties). |
| **Hunters** | 35% chance of one piece. |
| **Black Market** | Eight Fine-to-Legendary pieces, refreshed every 5 minutes. See [Economy](Economy). |
| **Stitching** | 10 leather + 10 mushrooms make a random piece at your level. See [Gathering and Crafting](Gathering-and-Crafting). |

Your race biases armor drops toward its own sets (62%). Bosses bias toward their element.

## Gear Inventory (I)

A dedicated window separate from the Bag (which holds consumables only):

- **Filter** by purpose (with counts), quality, element, slot, worn or locked.
- **Search** by name, set name or enchantment name.
- **Sort** by power for any purpose.
- **Sets tab**: groups set pieces, can auto-wear the best piece in each slot.
- **Stash**: disenchanted enchantments ready to apply.
- **Set & enchant totals**: your active set bonus, all enchantments summed up, and this class's set collection progress.

**Lock** a piece to protect it from selling. **Sell all unequipped** (optionally up to a quality) sells everything that isn't worn or locked.

## The Bag

Press **B**. The Bag holds **items only** (potions, brews, crafting materials, food, ghost jars, cleanse salts, etc.). Gear has moved to Gear Inventory (I).

## Disenchanting

Select a piece in Gear Inventory and **Disenchant** (80 kyo) to strip its last enchantment into your stash. From the stash you can set it onto another piece: a matching enchantment adds its power (up to that enchantment's maximum), otherwise it takes a new line (20 lines at most).

## Auto-equip

In TurboHunt, a new piece is worn automatically if it scores higher than what you have. In **Hardcore**, auto-equip is off — double-click a piece in Gear Inventory to wear it.
