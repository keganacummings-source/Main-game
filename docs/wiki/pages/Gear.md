# Gear

## In short

- Six slots: **weapon, head, chest, legs, feet, relic**.
- Gear always drops at **your level**. Better quality and better sources (bosses, bounties) push it a little higher.
- Six **qualities**: Worn, Common, Fine, Rare, Epic, Legendary. Higher quality means more stats, more enchantments and a higher level.
- Each piece has an **element**: it adds damage to matching verses, and your chest's element decides which enemy hits hurt more.
- A new piece is worn automatically if it scores higher than what you have. Use the **Bag** to compare, lock and sell.

## Slots and stats

| Slot | Gives |
| --- | --- |
| **Weapon** | Weapon damage = 8 + 2 × level + 6 × quality rank + Whetstone. Adds 35% of its damage to melee and ranged verses. +28% to verses of its element. |
| **Head, chest, legs, feet** | Armor = 8 + 2 × level + 5 × quality rank + Plating. +8% each to verses of their element. |
| **Relic** | Armor = 3 + level ÷ 2 + Plating. +8% to verses of its element. |

Quality rank: Worn 0, Common 1, Fine 2, Rare 3, Epic 4, Legendary 5.

Armor pieces (not weapons) belong to an **armor set**. Wear all five pieces of one set for its bonus. See [Sets](Sets).

**Gear score** (used for auto-equip and Account Score) = weapon damage × 4 + armor × 3 + (power + 12) for each enchantment.

## Gear level

A drop's level is **your level**, raised by its quality and its source. It never builds on the gear you already wear.

**Level = your level × (1 + quality % + source bonus + luck) + quality flat**

- Luck is at most +5%.
- Levels run from 1 to 1000.

## Enchantments

Each piece rolls **0–20 enchantments**: up to 12 from level (more on higher-level gear) and up to 8 from quality (Worn 0, Common 1, Fine 2, Rare 4, Epic 6, Legendary 8), ±1 at random.

Weapons favour weapon enchantments, armor favours armor ones, relics favour relic ones. Enchantment power grows with level and quality. See [Enchantments](Enchantments) for every one.

## Where gear comes from

| Source | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Monsters** | Chance = 12% + 0.3% per your level (up to level 100) + 8% per rarity + (Loot Find + Fortune) ÷ 200, at most 85%, × the foe's loot multiplier. The quality floor rises with rarity and at your levels 24 and 40. |
| **Bosses** | Always one piece (Fine, or Rare above level 14) plus an Epic-or-better piece. |
| **Shrines** | A Rare-or-better piece of the shrine's element. |
| **Evil witches** | Three Epic-or-better pieces. |
| **Bounties** | You choose one of six (Rare to Legendary by tier). See [Bounties](Bounties). |
| **Hunters** | 35% chance of one piece. |
| **Black Market** | Eight Fine-to-Legendary pieces, refreshed every 5 minutes. See [Economy](Economy). |
| **Stitching** | 10 leather + 10 mushrooms make a random piece at your level. See [Gathering and Crafting](Gathering-and-Crafting). |

Your race biases armor drops toward its own sets (62%), and bosses bias toward their element.

## The Bag

- **Compare:** each piece shows its level, stats, enchantment count, and the score change versus what you wear.
- **Lock** a piece to protect it from selling.
- **Sell all unequipped** (optionally only up to a quality) sells everything that isn't worn or locked.
- **Set & enchant totals** shows your active set bonus, every enchantment you are wearing summed up, and this class's set collection progress.
- The detail panel also names each piece's visual effect. See [Gear FX](Gear-FX).

## Disenchanting

For **80 kyo**, strip the last enchantment off the selected piece into your **stash**. Apply a stashed enchantment to another piece (up to 20 per piece). If the piece already has that enchantment, the powers add together, up to its maximum.
