# Bosses and Dungeons

## World bosses

Three warlords roam the overworld, and each dungeon has a boss in its last room.

<!-- gen:world-bosses -->

- A boss is always **at least 1–4 levels above you** (base level is a minimum).
- Bosses take **10 of your hits** (12 in Hardcore), hit for **7.5%** of your health, and have **3–4 traits**.
- **Rewards:**
  - an always-guaranteed gear drop (Fine floor, Rare above level 14);
  - an extra **Epic-or-better** piece (the "Warlord relic");
  - 4× XP and 6× kyo;
  - 28 Hunting experience.
- World bosses come back **70 s** after you beat them.

## The Tiny Crypt and the Ash Kiln

Two fixed dungeons on the overworld. Walk to the door and press **E**.

- **Tiny Crypt**: skeletons and ghouls, ending in the **Bone King**.
- **Ash Kiln**: fire spirits, ending in the **Ash Regent**.

Each visit is its own **instance**: foes you kill inside **stay dead** (they don't respawn, even if you reload the page in there). Walk out and back in to start a new instance with every foe back. The layout itself changes once per in-game day.

Leave through the exit at the entrance. In TurboHunt, dying inside only **knocks you out** (see [Death and Account](Death-and-Account)).

## Dungeon rifts

Every **10 minutes** of play (paused and hidden time don't count), a new **rift** opens somewhere near you. A message tells you, and the Map shows it.

- Walk within 120 px of the rift and press **E**, or use **Track** on the Map to walk there.
- Each rift is a **procedurally generated** dungeon in one of seven tile themes, and one of four layouts (below).
- Foes inside **don't respawn**. The **guardian** in the last room is a copy of one of the three world bosses (it hits 10% softer). Beat it to clear the rift, then leave through the entrance rift.
- Rifts stay open until cleared, and you can have several open at once.

## Dungeon layouts

Every dungeon — rifts, the Tiny Crypt and the Ash Kiln, in every mode that has them — rolls one of four layouts each day:

| Layout | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Halls** | The classic: rooms of mixed shapes joined by tunnels. |
| **Isles** | Islands in a lake (lava in the Kiln, black water in the Crypt) joined by stone causeways. **Each isle has its own tribe of foes** (fire, tide, grove, frost, storm, shade, bone or sun), with its own ground and scenery. The guardian waits on the farthest isle. |
| **Manor** | The inside of a two-storey house: rooms split by walls and doors, a great hall with a fountain, and a staircase (**UP** / **DOWN**) to the upper floor. Some houses have a second, servants' stair. The guardian waits in the deepest room upstairs. |
| **Portal maze** | Sealed chambers with no corridors. Step on a **PORTAL** pad to jump to its twin in another chamber. Every chamber is a different plane with its own floor, walls and foes. |

- Isles, manors and portal mazes put an **EXIT** sigil in the guardian's room that takes you straight back to the entrance.
- Pads glow and are labelled. Click-to-walk and autoplay path through them.
- The game names the layout when you walk in (for example *"Cinder Isles"*, *"Ossuary Manor"*, *"Shattered Sanctum"*).

## Shrines

Five elemental shrines are kept by NPCs: Light (Sister Ada), Earth (Sister Moss), Water (Cantor Brine), Ice (Warden Sleet) and Fire (Ash Cantor Vesper). Talk to the keeper to start a **3-round trial**:

| Round | Foe |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | A Saint of that element, your level + 2, +25% health |
| 2 | Your level + 4, +50% health |
| 3 | A **boss** Saint, your level + 6, +75% health |

- In TurboHunt you are fully healed before each round.
- **Reward:** a **Rare-or-better** piece of that element (+5% level bonus, high luck), plus kyo, plus 30 Adventuring experience.
- Dying in a shrine only knocks you out (TurboHunt).

## Evil witches

*TurboHunt only.* Every **20 levels** (20, 40, 60…), a rival witch comes for you with **two spirits**:

- She waits **18 seconds**, then hunts you down.
- She is your level + 2 (at least 22), with 3 traits and 4 verses.
- Beat her and she drops **everything**: three Epic-or-better pieces (+8% level bonus), her bag and a pile of kyo.

## The Green Cave

*Hardcore, level 30+.* Buy a **Green Mushroom** from the Black Market (sometimes in stock) and eat it to wake in a **fresh copy** of the Green Cave. It has its own map, foes, nodes and quest, and the quest starts over every time. Find the door marked **Wake up** to leave. See [Hardcore Survival](Hardcore-Survival).
