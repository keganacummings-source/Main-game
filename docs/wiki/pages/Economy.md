# Economy

**Kyo** is the currency.

## Earning kyo

- **Kills:** (2 + foe level), ×6 for bosses, × loot multiplier, × (1 + Kyo Magnet %) × (1 + class Fortune %).
- **Bounties and hunters:** big kyo rewards. See [Bounties](Bounties).
- **Quests and contracts.** See [Quests](Quests).
- **Selling** gear and materials.
- **Shrines:** kyo on top of the gear.

## Shops

| Where | Sells |
| --- | --- |
| **Quartermaster Holt** (near the hearth) | Potions, Big Potions, Ghost Jars, Cleanse Salt, Spirit Candy, Smoke Bombs (+ rations and cooked meat in Hardcore). Buys your material pack. |
| **Spore Broker Lise** | Opens Crafting for alchemy. |
| **Quiet Pike** (fence) and **Peddler Nessa** (armor) | Four random gear pieces at your level, rolled when you talk to them. |
| **Scribe Orien** | Opens your verse library. |
| **Black Market** (press **T** anywhere) | Gear, items, loose enchantments and spirits; refreshes every 5 minutes. |

### Buy prices

**Price = list price × max(50%, 1 − 0.125% per Trading level) × (1 − Shop Charm ÷ 140)**

### Sell prices

- **Gear** sells for **45%** of its value × (1 + 0.2% per Trading level) × (1 + Merchant Tongue %).
  - Gear value = 20 + 6 × level × quality rank (Worn 1 … Legendary 6) + 8 × total enchantment power.
- **Items** sell for 50% of their list price (at least 4) × the same multiplier.
- **Locked** and **worn** gear is never sold. **Sell all unequipped** in the Bag can stop at a quality you choose.

## The Black Market

Press **T** anywhere. The stock **changes every 5 minutes** (shown on the stall) and is the same for that window at your level.

| Tab | Stock | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Gear** | 8 pieces, Fine to Legendary, at your level +0–10% | Gear value × 1.35 |
| **Items** | 6 supplies (potions, jars, salt, candy, smoke, food); in Hardcore at level 30+, a **Green Mushroom** 40% of the time | 1.4–2.4 × list price (Green Mushroom: 300 + 15 × your level) |
| **Enchantments** | 5 loose enchantments (power 1–3) for your stash | 80–480 + 2 × level |
| **Spirits** | 4 spirits at your level +5 to +50 | 120 + 8 × spirit level + up to 200 |
| **Sell** | Sell any unworn gear or bag item | as above |

Shop prices are reduced by Trading and Shop Charm as above.

## Trading skill

Trading levels up by buying (+4), buying gear (+8), selling gear (+8) and selling the material pack (+10). Each level gives +0.2% sell price and 0.125% off shop prices (down to 50%).
