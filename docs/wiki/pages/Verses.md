# Verses

**Verses** are your attacks, spells, heals and movement skills. There are <!-- gen:verse-count --> in the game.

## In short

- You start with your class's verses. Each level gives **3 skill points**: open **Verses** (**V**) to unlock more or rank up the ones you have.
- **Ranks** go from 1 to 5. Each rank adds **+15%** to damage, heals and shields (rank 5 = ×1.6).
- Equip up to **9** verses. Only equipped verses are used, by you and by auto-battle.
- Every verse has an **element**, a **tactic** (how it plays) and a **form** (melee, ranged or spell).

## Reading a verse

| Stat | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Power** | Size of the hit (base damage 6 + 0.42 × power). Also lengthens the cooldown. |
| **Accuracy** | Chance to hit. |
| **Crit** | Base critical chance. |
| **Priority** | Speeds up the cooldown (0.08 s per point). Priority-2 verses can be cast while **silenced**. |
| **Effect** | Status left on the target. See [Status Effects](Status-Effects). |
| **Reach** | How far the verse reaches, in pixels (32 px = 1 tile). |
| **Base cooldown** | Seconds between casts with no Swift, Focus, Magic skill or Haste. See [Damage and Formulas](Damage-and-Formulas#cooldowns). |

### Forms

- **Melee**: short reach (44 px), ×1.35 damage, 20% shorter cooldown, adds your weapon, ignores armor.
- **Ranged**: long reach, adds your weapon, reduced by armor. Each ranged verse has a style:
  - **volley** fires 3 shots at 55% each;
  - **fast** is the plain style;
  - **long** adds +48 px of reach.
- **Spell**: long reach (at least 156 px), pure damage. Ignores the type chart, gear elements and armor. Scales with Magic skill (×1.45, +2.5% per level). Cooldown ×1.35, shortened by Magic and Focus.
