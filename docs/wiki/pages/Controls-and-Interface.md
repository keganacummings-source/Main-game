# Controls and Interface

## Keyboard and mouse

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **W A S D** / **arrow keys** | Move |
| **Left click** | Walk there; click a foe to go to it; click an NPC to talk |
| **Right click** | Inspect menu: details and actions for whatever is under the cursor |
| **Mouse over** | Hover card: a foe's level, health, traits, moves and what hits it harder |
| **Mouse wheel** | Zoom in and out |
| **1–9** (or numpad 1–9) | Cast the verse in that slot |
| **Numpad 0** / **Numpad Enter** | Attack the nearest foe in range with your best verse |
| **E** | Interact: talk, gather a node, fish, enter a dungeon or rift, use a bounty board |
| **B** or **I** | Bag |
| **V** | Verses (loadout and library) |
| **C** | Character sheet |
| **K** | Skills and account perks |
| **Q** | Quests (story, contract board, map quests) |
| **M** | Map |
| **G** | Spirits |
| **X** | Crafting |
| **L** | Lobby (multiplayer) |
| **T** | Black Market (buy and sell anywhere) |
| **H** | Survival panel (Hardcore) |
| **F** | Build mode (Hardcore) |
| **Esc** | Close a window or pause |

**Mashing keys** while asleep or rooted shakes the effect off faster. See [Status Effects](Status-Effects).

On phones and tablets a virtual stick appears for movement, and the HUD buttons open every window.

## The HUD

- **Top left, vitals:** level, name and class, kyo, health (with shield), experience, active effects with timers, your region and the latest log line.
- **Top centre, target:** the foe you are fighting, with level, element, health and traits.
- **Top right, tracker:** live bounties, Wanted level, quests, the clock and survival bars (Hardcore), Autoplay status.
- **Bottom, dock:** your 9 verse slots with cooldowns, plus buttons for every window.
- **Autoplay / Grind nodes:** your witch plays herself. In TurboHunt she grinds and fights; in Hardcore she gathers and fights.
- **Unstuck:** if you are wedged in a wall, press it, point the cursor at open ground, and after 3 seconds you land there.

## Nameplates and hover cards

Every foe on screen shows a nameplate, near or far:

- **Level badge** coloured by danger: teal (below you), gold (your level or up to 2 above), orange (3+ above), red (6+ above).
- **Name** and an **element dot**.
- **Rank tag**: BOSS, ★ BOUNTY (with tier), HUNTER or WITCH.
- **Trait tags**: short codes like VAMP, THRN, SWFT. See [Enemies](Enemies) for the full list.
- **Health bar** in quarters, with a pale trail that shows each hit.

Far-away plates fade, and crowded ordinary foes shrink to a slim bar. The foe you are fighting and the one under your cursor also show exact health.

Hover over anything for a card. Foes show what hits them harder, every trait with its full effect, their moves, and bounty details. NPCs, resource nodes and water show what you can do with them.

## Combat text

| Text | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| White number | Damage you dealt |
| Big yellow number with **CRIT** | A critical hit |
| Red number | Damage you took |
| Green **+number** | Healing |
| Grey MISS / DODGE / IMMUNE | Your hit missed, the foe dodged, or the element can't hurt it |
| DODGED / EVADE / CLEAR / GRAZE / CLIP | You avoided all or part of a hit |
| RESIST | An effect was blocked by immunity |
| SLEEP / ROOT / SILENCE / WAKE / AWAKE | Control effects starting and ending |
| SNAG / SLIP / TRIP | A defensive verse failed (see [Defense and Evasion](Defense-and-Evasion)) |
| ★ BOUNTY STAR | You earned a Bounty Star (see [Bounties](Bounties)) |

Numbers that pop on the same spot fan out instead of stacking.
