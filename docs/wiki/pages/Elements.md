# Elements

Every verse, monster, gear piece and class has one of **11 elements**.

## In short

- Hit a foe with an element it is **weak to** for **2×** damage. Hit it with one it **resists** for **½**. Some elements **can't be hurt** by others at all (0×).
- **Spells ignore the element chart.** They deal "pure" damage, so they are always safe.
- Every monster has a **hide**: *ethereal* hides take more from spells, *solid* hides take more from physical hits. The hover card tells you which.
- Wearing gear of your verse's element makes it hit harder.

## Type chart

Read across from the attacker. **2×** = super effective, ½ = resisted, **0** = no effect, · = normal.

## Hides: magic or physical

- **Ethereal hides** (Spirit, Void, Air, Electric, Light) take **×1.2** from spells and channels and **×0.8** from melee and ranged.
- **Solid hides** (Normal, Fire, Water, Earth, Ice, Ghoul) take **×1.2** from melee and ranged and **×0.8** from spells.

Your witch's auto-battle already prefers the matching kind (+28 to a verse's score).

## Elements in your gear

- **Weapon** of the verse's element: **+28%** damage.
- Each **armor piece** of the verse's element: **+8%** damage.
- **Typed Edge** enchantment: more again when the verse matches a worn piece.
- **Element Affinity** (class stat): bonus damage with your class's element.

Spells skip all of these, as they skip the type chart.

## Elements on defense

Enemy melee and ranged hits are checked against your **chest armor's element**:

- **×1.3** if the attack is strong against it;
- **×0.75** if weak;
- **×0.35** if your armor's element is immune to it.

Spells ignore this too. See [Defense and Evasion](Defense-and-Evasion).
