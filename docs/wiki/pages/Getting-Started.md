# Getting Started

Your first hour in TURBZ, step by step.

## 1. Make a witch

From the hub, pick **TurboHunt** (the normal mode) for your first game. [Hardcore](Game-Modes) is one life with survival rules, so save it for later.

Choose:

- **A name.**
- **A class.** This sets your element, stats, passive and starting verses. Any class works for a first game. Bruisers and Tanks are the most forgiving. See [Classes](Classes).
- **A trait** (optional). A small trade-off, like more health for less speed. See [Races and Traits](Races-and-Traits).

You wake at the **Crownfall hearth**, in the middle of the map.

## 2. Learn to move and fight

- Move with **WASD** or the **arrow keys**, or click the ground.
- Walk toward a monster. Once one of your verses can reach it, the fight starts.
- In TurboHunt your witch **attacks on her own**: she picks the best verse that can reach the nearest foe. You can also press **1–9** to cast a specific verse, or **0** on the numpad to attack.
- Watch for the red **!** over a monster. That is its wind-up. Step away (or out of the spot it aimed at) and the hit misses. See [Defense and Evasion](Defense-and-Evasion).

Your health refills on its own a few seconds after a fight (TurboHunt only).

## 3. First quest

Talk to **Caretaker Miret** at the hearth (walk up and press **E**, or click her). Her quest *First Steps* asks you to win 3 fights. It pays kyo, potions and ghost jars. The story quests after it walk you around the world. See [Quests](Quests).

## 4. Gear

Monsters drop gear. A new piece is **worn automatically** if it scores higher than what you have. Open the **Bag** (**B** or **I**) to compare, lock favourites and sell the rest. Gear always drops around **your level**, and better qualities sit a little above it. See [Gear](Gear).

## 5. Spend your points

Every level gives **3 skill points**. Open **Verses** (**V**) to unlock new verses or rank up the ones you use (each rank is +15% power, up to rank 5). You can equip up to **9 verses**. See [Leveling and Skills](Leveling-and-Skills).

## 6. Things to try next

- **Spirits**: some foes stay after you beat them and fight beside you. Crack a **Ghost Jar** to make it far more likely. See [Spirits](Spirits).
- **Contract Board**: ten small jobs that refresh every 30 minutes. See [Quests](Quests).
- **Bounties**: every tavern has a board with three marks to hunt. Big rewards, and you pick a piece of their gear. See [Bounties](Bounties).
- **Shrines**: talk to a shrine keeper for a 3-round fight with a guaranteed rare-or-better reward. See [Bosses and Dungeons](Bosses-and-Dungeons).
- **Dungeon rifts**: a new one opens every 10 minutes of play. See [Bosses and Dungeons](Bosses-and-Dungeons).

## Beginner tips

- **Match the hide.** Ethereal foes (Spirit, Void, Air, Electric, Light) take more from spells; solid foes take more from melee and ranged. Your witch already favours the right kind. See [Elements](Elements).
- **Read the nameplate.** The level badge turns orange or red when a foe is above you, and trait tags like **VAMP** or **THRN** warn you what it does. Hover over a foe for the full read.
- **Don't fear death too much (in TurboHunt).** Dying in a dungeon or shrine only knocks you out. A real death ends that witch, but your skills, account level, perks and finished sets stay. See [Death and Account](Death-and-Account).
- **Wearing matching elements** adds damage: a weapon of your verse's element adds +28%, each matching armor piece +8%.
