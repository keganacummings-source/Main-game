# Getting Started

Your first hour in TURBZ, step by step.

## 1. Make a witch

From the hub, pick **TurboHunt** (the normal mode) for your first game. [Hardcore](Game-Modes) is one life with survival rules — save it for later.

Choose:

- **A name.**
- **A class.** This sets your element, stats, passive and starting verses. Any class works for a first game. Tanks and Bruisers are the most forgiving. See [Classes](Classes).
- **A trait** (optional). A small trade-off, like more health for less speed. See [Races and Traits](Races-and-Traits).

You wake at the **hearth in Hearthmere's capital**, on the starting continent of the 500×500 Veilreach world.

## 2. Learn to move and fight

- Move with **WASD** or the **arrow keys**, or click the ground.
- Walk toward a monster. Once one of your verses can reach it, the fight starts.
- In TurboHunt your witch **attacks on her own**: she picks the best verse that can reach the nearest foe. You can also press **1–9** to cast a specific verse yourself, or **Numpad 0** to attack the nearest foe.
- Watch for the red **!** over a monster — that is its wind-up. Step away (or out of the spot it aimed at) and the hit misses. See [Defense and Evasion](Defense-and-Evasion).

Your health refills on its own a few seconds after a fight (TurboHunt only).

## 3. First quest

Talk to **Caretaker Miret** at the hearth (walk up and press **E**, or click her). Her quest *First Steps* asks you to win 3 fights and pays kyo, potions and ghost jars. The story quests after it walk you around the world. See [Quests](Quests).

## 4. Gear

Monsters drop gear. A new piece is **worn automatically** if it scores higher than what you have. Open **Gear Inventory** (**I**) to compare, lock favourites, sell the rest and track set progress. Gear always drops around **your level**, and better qualities sit a little above it. See [Gear](Gear).

## 5. Spend your points

Every level gives **3 skill points**. Open **Verses** (**V**) to unlock new verses or rank up the ones you use (ranks 1–5 are +15% power each; later ranks keep adding +8%). You can equip up to **9 verses**. See [Leveling and Skills](Leveling-and-Skills).

## 6. Things to try next

- **Spirits**: some foes stay after you beat them and fight beside you. Crack a **Ghost Jar** to make it far more likely. See [Spirits](Spirits).
- **Contract Board**: ten small jobs that refresh every 30 minutes. See [Quests](Quests).
- **Bounties**: every tavern has a board with three marks to hunt. Big rewards, and in TurboHunt you pick a piece of their gear. See [Bounties](Bounties).
- **Shrines**: talk to a shrine keeper for a 3-round fight with a guaranteed Rare-or-better reward. See [Bosses and Dungeons](Bosses-and-Dungeons).
- **Dungeon rifts**: a new one opens every 10 minutes of play near you. See [Bosses and Dungeons](Bosses-and-Dungeons).
- **The Map (M)**: explore the world — Hearthmere is just the beginning. Seven continents, 41 towns, and a main story called *The Sundered Crown* that sends you across all of them.

## Beginner tips

- **Match the hide.** Ethereal foes (Spirit, Void, Air, Electric, Light) take more from spells; solid foes take more from melee and ranged. Your witch already favours the right kind. See [Elements](Elements).
- **Read the nameplate.** The level badge turns orange or red when a foe is dangerous; trait chips like **VAMP** or **THRN** warn you what it does. Hover over a foe for the full card.
- **Gear Inventory vs Bag.** Press **I** for all your gear, sets and disenchanting. Press **B** for potions, materials and other items — they are now separate windows.
- **Don't fear death too much (in TurboHunt).** Dying in a dungeon or shrine only knocks you out. A real death ends that witch, but your skills, account level, perks and finished sets stay. See [Death and Account](Death-and-Account).
- **Wear matching elements.** A weapon matching your verse's element adds +28% damage; each matching armor piece adds +8%.
- **Watch for the aura menu.** Aura verses toggle on and off — they cost a share of your max health while active, but can be powerful. See [Verses](Verses#auras).
