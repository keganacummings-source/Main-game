# Classes

Your class decides your **element**, your **stats**, a **passive**, any **immunities**, your **starting verses** and how fast you learn each **skill**. There are <!-- gen:class-count --> classes in six roles.

## In short

- **Tanks** take hits. **Bruisers** hit hard up close. **Mobility** classes dart in and out. **Casters** nuke with spells. **Support** heals and buffs. **Marksmen** fight from range.
- Stats are percentages added on top of everything else (see the stat list below).
- Each class belongs to a **race** (from its element). That race's armor sets are more likely to drop for you.
- Finished armor sets unlock **per class**. See [Sets](Sets).

## Stats

<!-- gen:stat-keys -->

Class and trait stats add together. They feed into the formulas on [Damage and Formulas](Damage-and-Formulas):

- **Might** and **Arcana** multiply damage (never below ×0.2).
- **Guard** is capped at 60% damage reduction.
- **Haste** is capped between −40% and +45%.
- **Agility** multiplies movement speed (never below ×0.5).
- **Recovery** multiplies out-of-combat healing.
- **Lifesteal** heals that share of the damage you deal.
- **Fortune** and **Insight** multiply kyo and experience from kills (never below ×0.2).
- **Affinity** adds damage to verses of your class's element.

### Skill aptitudes

Aptitudes multiply the experience you earn in a skill. ×1.5 Hunting means every Hunting gain is 50% bigger; ×0.6 Mining means 40% smaller. See [Leveling and Skills](Leveling-and-Skills).
