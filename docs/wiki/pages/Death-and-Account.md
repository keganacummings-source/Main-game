# Death and Account

## In short

- **TurboHunt:** dying in a **dungeon, rift, shrine or designer map** only **knocks you out**. Dying in the **overworld** ends that witch.
- **Hardcore:** every death ends that witch.
- When a witch dies, her progress turns into **Account Score**. Your **skills, account level, perks and finished sets** stay.
- You then make the **next witch**, and can wake in a level-1 copy of a set that class has finished.

## Knock-outs (TurboHunt)

You lose a little kyo: **15% + 12**, at least 6 and never more than you have. You wake back at the dungeon or rift entrance with **42%** health, and keep all your gear.

## Real death

Your witch's run is banked as **Account Score**:

| Source | Score |
| --- | --- |
| Level | 50 per level |
| Experience | 1 per point toward the next level |
| Kyo | 1 per 2 kyo |
| Gear | each piece's gear score |
| Bag | 4 per item |
| Skills | 8 per skill level above 1 (Hardcore adds 24 more per level) |
| Spirits | each spirit's power (10 × level + 12 × rarity + speed + health) |

Your witch then joins the **Leaderboards** (the grave ledger) with a portrait of her last outfit.

## The next witch

On the rebirth screen you choose a name, class, trait and **spawn gear**:

- **Default robes:** a Common weapon of your element.
- **A finished set:** a level-1 copy of all five pieces plus a weapon, with the set bonus already on. Only sets **that class** has finished are offered. Class cards show how many sets each class has. See [Sets](Sets).

The new witch:

- starts at **level 1** in a random tavern (in Hardcore, at the **world's starting point** instead, see [Fast travel](Hardcore-Survival#fast-travel));
- keeps **85%** of the old kyo;
- gets a fresh bag (2 potions, plus 2 trail rations in Hardcore);
- gets a **fresh bounty board**, with no stars and no price on her head;
- keeps **every ghost**: your party and your Ghost Box, in TurboHunt and in Hardcore. When a witch dies her ghosts are banked on your account, and the next witch of that mode gets them back, even if you go back to the title screen and start a brand-new witch instead of choosing one on the death screen. The first ones join the party (up to 6), the rest wait in the Ghost Box.

In Hardcore, survival bars, the clock and map quests reset too.

## What stays with your account

| Kept through death | Lost with the witch |
| --- | --- |
| All 32 skills | Character level and experience |
| Account level, account XP and perk points | Verse ranks (back to your class's starting verses) |
| Perks | Gear and bag |
| Account Score | 15% of kyo |
| Ghosts in your party and Ghost Box | Bounty progress, stars and Wanted level |
| Finished sets (per class) | |
| Caught spirits | |

Skills, account level, perks and Account Score are shared between TurboHunt and Hardcore. Ghosts and finished sets belong to each mode: TurboHunt ghosts pass to your next TurboHunt witch, Hardcore ghosts to your next Hardcore witch.

## Second Chance

The **Second Chance** enchantment or perk saves you once per life: a killing blow leaves you at 1 health instead. It also puts out burning.
