# World and Regions

The overworld, in both TurboHunt and Hardcore, is **[Veilreach](#veilreach)**: a 500×500 world of seven continents split by sea. Every new hunter wakes at the hearth in Hearthmere's capital. After a death, TurboHunt wakes you at a random tavern, while Hardcore always sends you back to the starting point (see [Fast travel](#fast-travel-hardcore)). The classic sites (the hearth folk, taverns and their bounty boards, the five shrines, the Tiny Crypt and Ash Kiln doors, and the three world bosses) stand on Hearthmere, the starting continent. Visiting a new region for the first time gives Adventuring experience.

## The map (M)

Press **M** for the world map:

- **Pan and zoom.** Drag to pan, and use the mouse wheel, pinch or double-click to zoom. **−** and **+** zoom too. **Me** follows you again, and **Whole map** fits all of Veilreach.
- **Hover** a spot to read it: the area, its foe levels and description, and the nearest person or place.
- **Click** to drop a **pin**. A red arrow by your hunter points to it, with the distance in tiles. Click the pin again to remove it; it also clears when you arrive. In Hardcore, clicking a **ringed town** offers [fast travel](#fast-travel-hardcore) instead.
- **Find** a town, place or person by name and jump to it.
- **Show** turns layers on and off: areas, people, places, foes and resources. Resource nodes and ordinary foes only appear once you zoom in.
- **What it shows:**
  - **!** marks a quest offer (once you zoom in) and **?** a hand-in;
  - a pulsing gold ring is your next quest goal;
  - gold squares are towns, and in Hardcore a **white ring** marks a town you can fast travel to;
  - you'll also see bosses, bounty marks, rifts, campfires and your pin.
  - Arrows at the edge point to anything off-screen.

### Fast travel (Hardcore)

A new Hardcore witch always wakes at the **world's starting point** (the hearth in Hearthmere's capital). To get back to the places you have already found, open the map and click a town.

1. Press **M** and look for towns with a **white ring**. These are the capital you start in, plus every town this save has walked into.
2. Hover a ringed town: the tip reads **Click to fast travel**.
3. Click it. A prompt asks **Fast travel to *town*?** Choose **Travel** to jump there, **Pin instead** to drop a pin, or **×** to cancel.

Rules:

- Hardcore only, and only on the overworld. In a dungeon, rift or designer map there is nothing to click.
- Not while you are in a fight, taking hits or in a duel. Clear the enemies first.
- Only towns you have **walked into** are offered. A town you have only seen on the map has no ring.
- Visited towns are **kept when a witch dies**, so a new witch can skip the walk back out. Survival bars, the clock and map quests still reset. See [Death and Account](Death-and-Account#the-next-witch).
- Fast travel is free and has no cooldown.
- You arrive on the nearest open tile to the middle of the town.

TurboHunt has no fast travel. A dead witch wakes at a random tavern instead.

## Taverns

Every tavern has a **bounty board** by its door. In TurboHunt, a new witch wakes in a random tavern (never the same one twice in a row). In Hardcore she wakes at the world's starting point instead; see [Fast travel](#fast-travel-hardcore).

## NPCs

Talk to NPCs by walking up and pressing **E**, or clicking them. The first time you talk to each gives Social experience. Some can join you as **companions** ("Ask to follow" in the inspect menu, up to 3).

<!-- gen:npcs -->

Companions fight beside you:

- When a foe is close, they blink behind it (every 4 s).
- They hit it for 9 + 1.4 × your level up close, or 6 + 1.4 × your level from range.

## Landmarks

- **The hearth** (Hearthmere's capital): where a first witch wakes, and where every new Hardcore witch wakes. In TurboHunt it heals you fully.
- **Shrines**: five elemental trials. See [Bosses and Dungeons](Bosses-and-Dungeons#shrines).
- **Tiny Crypt** and **Ash Kiln**: fixed dungeons. See [Bosses and Dungeons](Bosses-and-Dungeons).
- **World bosses**: Tide Tyrant, Void Cantor and Marrow Saint.
- **Dungeon rifts**: open every 10 minutes near you.

## Veilreach

The overworld: 500×500 tiles, built with the [Map Builder](Designer-Mode#worlds) and editable there. You start in Hearthmere's capital.

| Continent | Theme | Foe levels (they overlap, so you can push ahead) |
|---|---|---|
| Hearthmere | green woods and fields | 1–12 (start) |
| Saltmire | coasts and marsh | 10–24 |
| Frostmarch | snow and ice | 20–36 |
| Emberwaste | ash and lava | 32–48 |
| Hollowdeep | void and dark stone | 45–62 |
| Bonereach | bone fields | 60–78 |
| Crownfall Isle | the ruined crown, in the middle | 75–90 |

- **Towns.** Each continent has a walled capital, towns, a port and an unwalled village. Towns have shops, a plaza and NPCs.
- **Ferries.** Walk to the end of a pier to sail to the next port. The ports form a ring, so every continent can be reached.
- **Wilds.** Woods, mines, fisheries and mushroom groves are full of gathering nodes. You will also find ruins, barrows, altars and watchstones, about 200 enemy camps, and a boss on each continent.
- **Areas.** Walking into a continent, town or landmark announces its name and levels. Your first visit gives a little Adventuring XP, and a first visit to a town or continent also trains **Wayfinding** (+30 and +20). Walking trains it too, and it makes you faster. Fast travel does not.
- **Story.** *The Sundered Crown* sends you to every capital for a shard of the crown, then to Crownfall Isle. Each town also has three side quests: clearing roads, supply runs and letters. See [Quests](Quests#map-quests).

## Designer maps

Maps built in the [Map Builder](Designer-Mode) can be entered from the game. They have their own spawn zones, NPCs, stories and quests. In TurboHunt, dying on one only knocks you out.
