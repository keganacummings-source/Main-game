# Spirits

Some foes **stay with you** after you beat them, as spirits that follow and fight beside you.

## In short

- After a kill, there is a chance the foe stays (**catching**). Its ghost **lingers** where it fell for 5 minutes. **Ghost Jars** make it much more likely for a minute.
- Up to **3 ghosts are out** with you at once.
- Your **party holds 6**. Swap who is out in **Spirits** (**G**).
- Everything else lives in your **Ghost Box**, a chest you place at your camp. Stand by it to store, take, swap and release ghosts.
- **Shiny ghosts:** 1 in 100 ghosts is shiny. Its colours flow like a lava lamp, it circles you while it follows, and it is a little livelier in a fight.
- Each spirit knows up to 10 verses and readies **3**. Healers will heal you, cleansers will cleanse you, and the rest attack.

## Catching

### Lingering ghosts

A caught ghost does not join straight away. It stands where the foe fell, with its name, level and a timer, so you can finish the fight first. Walk up and press **E**, or click it, to open its window:

- **Stats:** health, speed, rarity, element, FX and every move it knows (readied moves are marked ●).
- **Rename** it (up to 20 letters).
- **Add to party:** it follows if fewer than 3 ghosts are out, otherwise it waits in the party (6 at most).
- **Send to Ghost Box:** needs a built Ghost Box; works from anywhere.
- **Dismiss:** let it go.

Unclaimed ghosts fade after **5 minutes** and blink in the last 30 seconds. Catching XP and catch counts are given when you keep the ghost. With autoplay on, ghosts join on the spot as before.

When a foe falls, the chance it stays is:

**12% + 0.12% per Catching level + Catch Boost ÷ 220 + 3% per rarity step + 45% while a Ghost Jar is active**

There is no cap. Past a sure catch, the extra chance raises the odds that the ghost is shiny and gives it more health.

- **Can't be caught:** animals, bosses, witches, shrine Saints, and anything summoned by another foe.
- A **Ghost Jar** lasts 60 seconds. Use it from the Bag before a fight.
- The new spirit follows straight away if you have a free slot; otherwise it waits in the party.
- Catching trains the **Catching** skill and counts for catch contracts and some story quests.

## Your spirits

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Health | 72% of the caught foe's max health (at least 18) |
| Level and rarity | the foe's |
| Verses known | up to 10, from its element and Spirit |
| Verses readied | 3 (choose in Spirits) |

### What spirits do in a fight

Ghosts fight on the same system you do: each readied verse has its own cooldown (1.5× your base cooldown) and range, and a ghost only casts what reaches. When a ghost is ready it chooses:

1. A **heal** if you are under 50% health.
2. A **cleanse** (70% of the time) if you have any effect on you.
3. A **ward** if you have no ward and are under 80%.
4. An 18% chance of a **surge** (speed and crit for you).
5. Otherwise an **attack** at your target that is ready and in reach (stronger verses are picked more often). Ghost hits use the ghost's own level (never above yours) and rarity, not your verse ranks or class, and deal 0.62 × (0.75 + 0.12 × rarity) of normal damage. Melee verses send the ghost darting in.

Foes aim 28% of their attacks at your spirits. A spirit at 0 health drops out of the fight and recovers **6 health per second** when the fight ends.

## Managing spirits

In **Spirits** (**G**):

- **Follow / wait**: choose which spirits follow you (up to your slot count).
- **Ready verses**: pick 3 of each spirit's known verses (at least 1).
- **Dismiss** a spirit you no longer want.
- **Box**: spirits bought from the Black Market wait in the box until you call them into the party.

Ghost slots no longer exist. Any skill points spent on them were refunded.

## The Ghost Box

- **Hardcore:** build it inside a camp (a campfire's safe zone) from **Survival & Camp → Build**: 4 Logs and 4 Vein Ore. Click it in build mode to pick it up.
- **TurboHunt:** place it anywhere in the overworld from **Spirits** (same cost).
- When your party is full, send a lingering ghost to the box instead. Without a box, make room in the party first or the ghost fades.
- The box holds up to 300 ghosts. One per hunter.

## Spirit power

For [Account Score](Death-and-Account) each spirit counts 10 × level + 12 × rarity + speed + health.
