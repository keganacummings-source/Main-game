# Spirits

Some foes **stay with you** after you beat them, as spirits that follow and fight beside you.

## In short

- After a kill, there is a chance the foe stays (**catching**). Its ghost **lingers** where it fell for 5 minutes. **Ghost Jars** make catching far more likely for 60 seconds.
- Up to **3 ghosts are out** with you at once.
- Your **party holds 6**. Swap who is out in **Spirits** (**G**).
- Everything else lives in your **Ghost Box** — a chest you build at your camp. Stand by it to store, take, swap and release ghosts. It holds up to **300** ghosts.
- **Every kind of foe can leave a ghost:** monsters, animals, witches, hunters, bounty marks, shrine foes and summoned foes. **Bosses** leave a ghost half the time.
- **Rarity:** Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic. Rarer ghosts hit harder, roll more abilities and look it.
- **Abilities:** every ghost rolls 1–3 abilities. They work while the ghost is **out with you** or **tagged** as a tiny follower.
- **Ghosts level up:** ghosts out with you and tagged ghosts earn XP from every kill, up to **10 levels past you**.
- **Shiny ghosts:** 1 in 100 ghosts is shiny (1 in 50 for bosses, ×1.3 at night in Hardcore). Shinies wear one of 12 palettes and are livelier. They are animated in **Shinies Seen** on the main menu.
- Each spirit knows up to 10 verses and readies **3**. Healers will heal you, cleansers will cleanse you, the rest attack.

## Catching

### Lingering ghosts

A caught ghost does **not** join straight away. It stands where the foe fell for up to 5 minutes, so you can finish the fight first. Walk up and press **E**, or click it, to open its window:

- **Overview:** a live portrait, rarity and palette badges, health, XP and its full stats.
- **Abilities:** each ability with its exact numbers, quality and cooldown.
- **Verses:** every verse it knows (readied verses are marked ●).
- **Rename** it (up to 20 letters).
- **Add to party:** it follows if fewer than 3 are out; otherwise it waits in your party (6 max).
- **Send to Ghost Box:** needs a built Ghost Box; works from anywhere in the overworld.
- **Dismiss:** let it go.

The ghost **blinks** in the last 30 seconds before it fades. Catching XP and catch counts are given when you keep it.

With autoplay on, ghosts join on the spot as before.

### Catch chance

**12% + 0.12% per Catching level + Catch Boost ÷ 220 + 3% per rarity step + 45% while a Ghost Jar is active**

There is no cap. Past a sure catch, the excess raises shiny odds and gives the ghost more health.

- **Every foe can be caught:** animals, witches, hunters, bounty marks, shrine foes and summoned foes use this chance too.
- **Bosses** use their own roll: a shiny ghost 1 time in 50, otherwise an ordinary ghost half the time. Boss ghosts are built at ghost size.
- A **Ghost Jar** lasts 60 seconds.
- The **Catching** skill and **Catch Boost** enchantment raise the base chance.
- Catching trains the **Catching** skill and counts for catch contracts.

### What spirits do in a fight

Ghosts fight on the **same system you do**: per-verse cooldowns (1.5× your base cooldown), range checks and real strikes, at the ghost's own level and rarity. When a ghost is ready it chooses, in order:

1. A **heal** if you are under 50% health.
2. A **cleanse** (70% of the time) if you have any effect on you.
3. A **ward** if you have no ward and are under 80%.
4. An 18% chance of a **surge** (speed and crit for you).
5. Otherwise an **attack** at your target that is ready and in reach (stronger verses favoured).

Ghost hits deal 0.62 × (0.75 + 0.12 × rarity) of normal damage. Melee verses send the ghost darting in. Foes aim **28%** of their attacks at your spirits.

A spirit at 0 health drops out of the fight and recovers **6 health per second** once the fight ends.

### Ghost XP

Every kill gives each ghost **out with you** 60% of the kill's base XP and each **tagged tiny follower** 35%. A ghost needs 60 + 1.2 × level² XP for its next level and gains health as it grows. Ghosts can grow **10 levels past you**, so they always have room to gain. In Hardcore, **Go Leveling** still trains a party ghost alone at 2× XP, up to the same cap.

## Rarity

Foes are Common to Epic. A ghost starts at its foe's rarity, then may climb: an Epic ghost becomes **Legendary** 6% of the time (35% from a boss), and a Legendary one becomes **Mythic** 4% of the time (15% from a boss). A shiny gets one more 25% chance to climb a tier.

Every rarity has its own look: a colour wash over the body, a glow underneath and its own opacity; from Rare up a gleam sweeps across the body now and then. Mythic ghosts also shed sparks.

## Shiny palettes

A shiny rolls one of twelve palettes. **Prism** is the original rainbow (every shiny from before palettes keeps it). Rarer ghosts are likelier to roll the rarer palettes.

## Abilities

Each ability has a **quality** (0–100%) rolled with it; strength grows with the ghost's level (×1.06 at level 1, ×1.6 at 100, ×2.2 at 400) and rarity. Abilities only work while the ghost is **out with you** or **tagged**. Timed abilities start on a **full cooldown** when they switch on, so untagging and retagging gives no free use.

Bonuses from many ghosts **stack up to each ability's cap**. A timed ability several ghosts share runs on one timer: it uses the best value plus half of each other one (damage at most twice the best) and recharges 10% faster per extra ghost (at most 40% faster). The **Bonuses** tab in Spirits shows your totals and timers.

### Ghost FX

Each ghost's visual effect is read from the ghost itself:

- **Element** particles and colour.
- **Rarity**: particle count, crest and glow intensity.
- **Level**: particle spread.
- **Speed**: pace and a speed streak.
- **Health**: a guard ring.
- **Shiny**: its palette's lava-lamp colours and a lively, zippy movement pattern.

Rare+ and shiny ghosts **imbue** their own verses (an extra visual layer on casts). The Spirits window describes each ghost's FX.

### Tiny followers

Tag up to **20 Ghost Box ghosts** (Spirits → Ghost Box → Tag) to float with you at quarter size, on your shoulders or overhead. They never fight, but their **abilities work** and they **earn XP** from your kills. Tagging works from anywhere.

## Managing spirits

In **Spirits** (**G**):

- **Follow / wait**: choose which spirits are out (up to 3).
- **Ready verses**: pick 3 of each spirit's known verses.
- **Dismiss** a spirit you no longer want.
- **Hold followers**: send spirits and companions to wait at your campfire's safe zone.
- **Party / Ghost Box / Bonuses** tabs. Open any ghost for its portrait, stats, abilities and verses.
- **Tiny followers**: tag up to 20 Ghost Box ghosts to float with you at quarter size (abilities on, earns XP).

## The Ghost Box

| Mode | How to place |
| --- | --- |
| **Hardcore** | Build from **Survival & Camp → Build**: 4 Logs, 4 Vein Ore. Must be placed inside a camp (campfire safe zone). |
| **TurboHunt** | Spirits (G) → Ghost Box → **Place here** (4 Timber + 4 Ore) or **Buy & place** (150 kyo). |

**Move Ghost Box here** brings it to you from anywhere on the overworld once placed.

When your party is full, send a lingering ghost to the box instead. Without a box, make room in the party first or the ghost fades.

Right-clicking ground also offers **Place / Move Ghost Box here** in TurboHunt.

## Witch and bounty ghosts

**Evil witches** and **bounty marks** bring real ghosts of their own: ghost kits, aid verses (heals and wards), the same shiny roll. They use them exactly as you do. Their ghosts enrage when they do.

## Ghost floats

Spirits pick their own spots around you, drift on momentum, trail when you walk and catch up. Shinies are restless little sparks that zip and loop but stay close.

## Spirit power

For [Account Score](Death-and-Account) each spirit counts 10 × level + 12 × rarity + speed + health.
