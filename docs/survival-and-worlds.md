# Verses, survival and rebuilt worlds

Right-click or open Menu → Verses. Hover, keyboard-focus, or tap an ability name
for its description, power, accuracy, critical chance, range and effect. Unlocks
and ranks still use the character's skill points.

## Hardcore frontier

Hardcore keeps its separate save slot and now generates a different wilderness.
Witch parties and the old answer-only duel loop no longer run. Attacks and healing
spells can be cast freely using your unlocked abilities.

- Full Hunger lasts 30 active minutes; Thirst 20; Sleep 40.
- Empty reserves cause 0.4 / 0.8 / 0.2 damage per second respectively.
- Sleep below 15 slows movement by 30%.
- Pause, death, title screen and hidden browser tabs stop the simulation.
- Injuries persist. Passive regeneration, meals, potions, level-ups, hearths,
  shrine entry and shrine rounds cannot restore your health. Healing spells
  (including spirit healing and spell leech) can.
- Death takes the current body even inside a dungeon or shrine. Reload retains
  death and offers rebirth. Rebirth resets supplies and starts with two rations.

Open **Survival & Camp** from the HUD or right-click menu:

1. Buy rations/cooked meat from Quartermaster Holt, or hunt animals for raw meat.
2. Stand within 64 pixels of a LOGS resource and chop, or right-click its tree.
3. Build a campfire with 3 logs on open ground. Campfires persist in your save.
4. Cook one raw meat or fish using one extra log.
5. Stand within 80 pixels of a water tile, then harvest raw water (2 seconds).
6. At a campfire, purify one raw water using one extra log.
7. Use meals and drinking water from Bag. Raw meat/fish cannot heal or be eaten
   uncooked in Hardcore. Food and water restore needs, not injuries.
8. Sleep within 80 pixels of a campfire. A full rest takes about 13 active seconds;
   food/water keep draining. Enemies or damage interrupt sleep. Wake manually
   with the HUD button. Sleep does not heal.

Cooking/purification fail without consuming anything if materials or fire
proximity are missing. Moving away cancels tree gathering and water harvesting.
Campfires remain lit; logs fuel each cooking/purification action.

## World rebuild

Both modes use new seeded terrain: winding rivers, lakes, clustered forest,
resource groves, roads and shelters, with supplied pixel terrain/tree PNGs.
Hardcore changes regional placement, river basin, lakes, forest density and
settlement layout. Existing keeper, shrine, dungeon and tavern locations stay
connected. A starter grove and small pond near the hearth support the first
survival loop. Existing characters retain progress and relocate to open ground
if their saved position is blocked by the rebuilt terrain.
